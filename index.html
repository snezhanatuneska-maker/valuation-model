Free startup valuation for early-stage founders

Find out what your company could be worth before you talk to investors, and see exactly how that number is reached.

  • What you getA pre-money and post-money valuation blended from four standard methods (Scorecard, Venture Capital, Comparables, discounted cash flow), a check against how a typical funding round at your stage would price you, a 5-year projection, a revenue sensitivity check, and a PDF report that explains every number.
  • How long it takesAbout 15 minutes if you have your last 12 months' figures and a simple 5-year plan at hand. Or see a finished example in one click.
  • Where the data comes fromIndustry benchmarks and country risk from Prof. Aswath Damodaran (NYU Stern, 2026), German tax law including the 2028–2032 corporate tax cuts, the 10-year Bund yield, and Equidam's 2026 early-stage valuation data. Every source is listed in the report.

An informed starting point for investor conversations, not a certified appraisal.

Privacy in this demo

  • Nothing is stored. What you enter is sent to the calculation server only to work out your result and build your PDF, and is discarded straight after. There is no database and no account.
  • Where it runs. This page is hosted on GitHub Pages and the calculation server on Render (render.com). Like any web host, they may keep short-lived technical logs, such as your IP address and the time of a request, to run and protect their service.
  • No tracking. No analytics, no advertising, no cookies, and nothing loaded from other websites. The page doesn't save anything in your browser.
  • Your logo, if you add one, is only used to build your PDF and isn't kept.
